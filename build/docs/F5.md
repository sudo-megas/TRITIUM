# TRITIUM — F5 · v0.1.5 · «Costs»

Repo path of this file: `~/REPO/build/docs/F5.md`
Governing document: `~/REPO/XTRITIUM.md` — where this file and XTRITIUM disagree, XTRITIUM wins.

F4 was about a figure that exists in no file. F5 is about three that have been
sharing one.

The MAKRO SATIN ALIM sheet — `draft/CUSTOM-ANOTHER-COST.jpg`, thirteen rows,
every non-fuel lira this vehicle has cost — carries a column headed AÇIKLAMA.
What is in it is `Enpara / Taksit 6`, `Enpara / Sonradan Taksitlendirme 6`,
`World Sanal`, and once a genuine remark: `Ecem ödedi.` A bank, an instalment
plan and a note, divided by a slash where the maker remembered one and by nothing
where he did not. That column cannot be sorted, cannot be summed, and cannot be
asked which card paid for the Kasko.

XTRITIUM §4.4 already ruled on it. `bank` is its own field, and the reason is
written into the constitution beside it — "totals by bank become possible".
`instalment` is its own field too, "plain text, no engine behind it". F5 is where
those stop being lines in a specification.

The rest of the milestone is §6 transcribed: the category tree of §6.1, the form
that changes shape with the category (§6.2), and the three money fields. It adds
no new way to disk and no new kind of window — F2 wrote `costs.toml`, and F3 and
F4 built every piece of machinery a form window needs.

### 1.4 What Fuelio does here, and what TRITIUM does not

`draft/ADD-ANOTHER-COST.jpg` is Fuelio's Add cost screen. TRITIUM keeps the
category picker, the title, the total, the date and the negative-cost toggle. It
drops four things, none of them by oversight:

- **Time.** §4.4's `date` is a TOML local date. A payment has a day; the minute it
  happened is not a figure anything in TRITIUM computes.
- **Save as template.** A template exists to produce an entry the maker did not
  make. §3.3 forbids exactly that.
- **Recurrence.** §3.3 again, and by name: "no recurrence engine". TEKRAR EDEN is
  a *category*, not a schedule — Kasko recurs in the world, and TRITIUM records
  each year's payment when it is made.
- **The odometer field.** Fuelio puts one on every cost. In TRITIUM only Periyodik
  Bakım carries an odometer (§4.4's `service.toml`), and that is F6's.

`draft/ADD-ANOTHER-COST-TYPES.jpg` shows Fuelio's category dropdown: nine flat
entries — Servis, Bakım, Kayıt, Park, Yıkama, Geçiş Ücreti, Bilet/Ceza, Modifiye,
Sigorta — every one of which §6.1 drops. It also shows the difference F5 must
express: **Fuelio's categories are a flat list; TRITIUM's are a tree.**

`draft/LIST-OTHER.jpg` is Fuelio's empty cost log: an illustration, "No data for
this vehicle", "You need to add some data first." It is the "get started" screen
§7 forbids by name. F5's empty pane is the filled pane holding nothing.

## 2. SCOPE — IN

### 2.1 The decisions this milestone settles

1. **One form, not two.** §5.1's quick-add/full-form pair is a *fuel* decision: a
   fill-up has three figures worth a fast path, and the fields it does not ask
   have sane defaults. A cost has no such subset. §6.2's whole point is that the
   form *changes shape* with the category, so a quick path would have to pick one
   shape and be wrong for the others. F5 ships `cost` as a single form kind.

2. **The picker is the tree, not a list.** Group first — İLK ALIŞ · TEKRAR EDEN ·
   MANUAL — and category second, from `COST_CATEGORIES`, which already holds §6.1
   exactly. Changing the group clears the category rather than carrying a nonsense
   pair forward. This is the one place TRITIUM's shape visibly differs from
   Fuelio's, and it is deliberate: the group is what the form adapts to.

3. **Periyodik Bakım is not F5's to write.** It sits in §6.1's TEKRAR EDEN column,
   but §6.2 sends its entries to `service.toml`, and F6 is «Service — Periyodik
   Bakım shape, vendor-as-text». The cost form writes `costs.toml`; a category
   whose entries do not live there does not belong in its picker. So **F5's tree
   is §6.1's tree minus that one category**, and F6 gives it its own entry path,
   exactly as F4 gave fuel one. `records.ts` already carries the comment saying
   why. §6.1's tree is therefore expressed across two tabs — which is what §6.2
   implied the moment it put those entries in a different file.

4. **MANUAL's category is typed, and stored as a slug.** §6.1 gives `manual` no
   fixed categories, and `COST_CATEGORIES.manual` is `[]` deliberately — it is
   "add custom: …". The maker types the category; it is stored through `slugFor`
   so that `Lastik` and `lastik` are one category and not two. Totals by category
   are F9's and F10's, and they will be wrong from the first day if the key is
   whatever case the maker was typing in. `slugFor` moves out of `repository.ts`
   into `src/shared/slug.ts` — it is already transliteration-correct for Turkish
   and locale-independent by construction (§3.6), and F5 is its second caller
   rather than a second implementation of it.

5. **`payment_method` ships fixed, and never loses an unknown value.** §4.4 calls
   it an "editable list" and ships three: EFT, kredi kartı, banka kartı. Editing
   the list is a settings surface, and **F11 owns settings whole** — the same
   reason F4 refused to ship half a unit system. So F5 renders the three the way
   F4 renders `fuel_type`, and a value already in the file that is not among them
   **is shown as its own option** instead of being silently reset on the next
   edit. §3.8 — the maker's word is final, including a word typed into Neovim.

6. **`bank` and `instalment` are free text.** XTRITIUM fixes no bank vocabulary,
   and the sheet shows why one cannot be invented: `Enpara` is an institution and
   `World Sanal` is a card product, and both are correct answers to "which account
   paid". §4.4 asks only that `bank` be its own field so that totals by bank
   become possible, which a free string already satisfies. `instalment` is free
   text by §4.4's own words — "plain text, no engine behind it" — and `Taksit 6`
   and `Sonradan Taksitlendirme 6` are both kept as written, because they are not
   the same arrangement and only the maker knows it.

7. **`income` is the sign, and the amount stays positive on disk.** §4.4 calls
   these "negative costs (payouts, refunds)". The file stores a positive `amount`
   beside `income = true`; storing a negative amount as well would be two sources
   of truth for one fact, which §3.7 forbids by name. The pane renders the figure
   negated — `-11.746,00 ₺`, through the `formatFigure` that already knows how —
   and F9's lifetime totals subtract it. Nothing on disk changes sign. The form
   forces the amount positive on the way out, and so does the main process at the
   boundary: a minus typed into the field is read as emphasis, not as data.

8. **A cost is added by the main process.** `cost:add(slug, entry)` reads
   `costs.toml`, allocates the id, appends and writes — the same reasoning F4 gave
   for `fuel:add`: a form window that has been open for an hour cannot hand back a
   whole stale document and lose what was written meanwhile. Editing an existing
   cost keeps a by-id `cost:update`. Both mirror `addFuelEntry` and
   `updateFuelEntry`, and share `allocateId`.

9. **The cost write broadcasts — and F5 repairs a save that never did.** F4's
   fourth decision made every fuel write tell the other windows. It did not reach
   costs: `ipcMain.handle('costs:save')` in `src/main/index.ts` is the one write
   path in the process with no `broadcast('vehicles:changed')` after it. Without
   it a cost saved in a form window would never reach the shell, which would go on
   showing the file as it was when it last rendered. F5 fixes the costs channel.
   **`service:save` has the same gap and F6 fixes that one** — recorded here so it
   is inherited rather than discovered.

10. **Today is built from the local calendar.** `todayIso()` already exists and is
    already right about this — `getFullYear`/`getMonth`/`getDate`, never
    `toISOString().slice(0, 10)`, because this machine is UTC+3 and a cost entered
    at 01:00 would otherwise be filed on yesterday. `audit-locale` cannot catch it,
    because it is not a locale call. F5 **moves it from `fuel-draft.ts` into
    `format.ts`**, beside the other date handling, rather than importing fuel code
    into cost code.

11. **Costs sort by date, newest first.** A cost has no odometer, so F4's
    `sortByOdometer` does not apply to it. Equal dates fall back to id descending,
    which makes the order total: the maker entered Trafik Sigortası 26/27 and Kasko
    26/27 on the same eleventh of April, and they must not swap places between
    renders.

12. **F5 deletes nothing.** A mis-entered cost is edited. Removing one belongs with
    F7, where the list lives — F4 said the same about a fill-up, and the plaintext
    promise means a text editor can do it today.

13. **The labels live in the catalogue; the slugs live in the file.** §4.4's own
    sample stores `category = "trafik-sigortasi"`, so a display label is needed
    whatever else is decided. It goes in the i18n catalogue with the same Turkish
    text in `en.json` and `tr.json`: `audit-strings` stays satisfied, the maker's
    own nouns stay as he wrote them in both languages, and whether "Kasko" ever
    gets an English wording remains a decision that can be made later instead of
    one baked into a `.tsx`.

### 2.2 The tree — `src/shared/records.ts`

Transcribed in F2 and unchanged by this milestone:

```
İLK ALIŞ            TEKRAR EDEN          MANUAL ENTRY
├─ Kapora           ├─ MTV 1             └─ add custom: …
├─ Araç Bedeli      ├─ MTV 2
├─ Noter (Ruhsat)   ├─ Trafik Sigortası
├─ Plaka (Noter)    └─ Kasko
└─ Plaka (Ş.O)
```

TEKRAR EDEN shows four of its five: Periyodik Bakım is F6's, per decision 3. What
F5 adds is the pair of helpers the form needs — the categories offered for a
group, and whether a group takes a typed category — and nothing else.

The maker's thirteen rows map onto it without remainder, which is the check that
matters: Kapora, Araç Satın Alım Bedeli, Noter Ruhsat, Plaka Noter and Plaka
Şöförler Odası into İLK ALIŞ; 2025 MTV 2, 2026 MTV 1, 2026 MTV 2, Trafik Sigortası
25/26 and 26/27, Kasko 25/26 and 26/27, and Kasko 26/27 Zeyil1 into TEKRAR EDEN.
The year in each of those is the **title**, not the category — which is what makes
`Kasko 26/27 Zeyil1` an ordinary second entry in a category rather than a special
case.

### 2.3 The form — `src/renderer/forms/CostForm.tsx`

A window of its own (§5.1 — movable, non-anchored), opened empty for a new cost or
on an existing `entry` id to edit one (§3.8). `FormRequest` already carries both
`slug` and `entry`; F5 adds no field to it.

The shape §6.2 asks for:

- **Always:** group, category, title, amount, date, the income toggle, note.
- **İLK ALIŞ and TEKRAR EDEN — the money categories:** payment method, bank and
  instalment appear.
- **MANUAL:** the money shape, with the category typed instead of picked.
- **Periyodik Bakım:** part, odometer and vendor, into `service.toml` — F6's.

Worth saying plainly rather than leaving to be noticed: **in F5 the form's only
visible adaptation is the category control.** §6.2 gives MANUAL "the money
shape", which is the shape İLK ALIŞ and TEKRAR EDEN already wear, so the payment
fields are present for all three groups. The one branch whose shape genuinely
differs is the one this milestone does not write. The machinery is here and the
seam is where §6.2 put it; F6 hangs the second shape on it.

The amount is entered and stored as entered; there is no derived figure on this
form, which is the one way it is simpler than the fuel form. A date that is not
`GG/AA/YYYY` warns and saves empty, as the vehicle and fuel forms already do.

**Save is refused until there is an amount and a category** — what it cost, and
what kind of thing it was. That is the same gate the fuel form applies to
odometer and litres, and it is not a validation rule in the §3.8 sense: a warning
tells the maker something looks wrong and then accepts his word, whereas a cost
with neither figure is not a record of anything yet.

### 2.4 The pane — `src/renderer/panes/CostsPane.tsx`

Provisional, in F4's pattern: one button, and a list of the vehicle's costs —
date, group, category, title, amount, payment method, bank, instalment — reading
`bundle.costs.entries` from the store that already holds the whole vehicle. No
second read of the file, no second store.

Income renders negated (decision 7). The empty case is a sentence inside the
table, with the headers and the column widths exactly where a filled app would put
them (§7).

### 2.6 One thing found while running it

Establishing a green baseline before touching anything turned up a failure that
had nothing to do with costs: `shell.spec.ts` › *the window refuses to shrink
below 1280 × 720* fails on the v0.1.4 tree, reproducibly, alone or in the suite.

It is a race in the test, not a defect in the app. `launchApp` resolves when the
Electron process is up, which is **before** `app.whenReady` has fired and
`createWindow` has run. Every other test in that file happens to `await
app.firstWindow()` on its first line and so waits without meaning to; this one
goes straight to `BrowserWindow.getAllWindows()` and finds the list empty. It
would have passed on a machine where the app started faster, which is the worst
property a test can have — the guarantee it exists to defend (§7's minimum size)
was going unchecked, and the red was being read as noise.

F5 awaits the window first. A one-line fix, recorded here rather than slipped in,
because it changes what the suite was actually proving.

## 4. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA

F5 is done when every line below is true:

1. `npm test` is green: the five audits, both tsconfigs silent under `strict`, the
   units, the build, and the e2e suite.
2. A cost entered through the form lands in `vehicles/<slug>/costs.toml` as one
   `[[entry]]` in the §4.4 shape, with an allocated `id`.
3. The group picker offers İLK ALIŞ, TEKRAR EDEN and MANUAL; the category picker
   offers §6.1's categories for the chosen group, **without Periyodik Bakım**; and
   MANUAL takes a typed category and stores it slugified.
4. `bank` and `instalment` are separate keys in the file, and a row of the maker's
   own sheet — `Enpara / Taksit 6` — round-trips into two fields and back.
5. `income = true` leaves `amount` positive on disk and shows the figure negated.
6. An unknown `payment_method` in a hand-edited file survives an edit of the same
   entry.
7. The shell shows a cost saved in a form window without being restarted.
8. The form is a genuine second window, movable outside the main one, and
   `window.open` is still refused.
9. Every figure renders in the family convention — `1.234,56 ₺`, `GG/AA/YYYY` — in
   both languages, with no `Intl` call and no locale read.
10. A figure typed into the form, saved, and reopened is the same figure.
11. `writeFileSync` appears nowhere in `src/` outside `atomic.ts`.
12. Zero network: the audits still prove it statically and the offline e2e run
    still behaves identically.
13. No AI attribution exists anywhere in the tree or the git history.
